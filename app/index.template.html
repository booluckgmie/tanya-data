About this prototype

Reliability tiers. Tier 1 is official statistics from the national statistics office or the central bank. Tier 2 is official administrative data from a ministry or agency. Tier 3 is data whose publisher is a company, an international body or an independent project (for example ElectionData.MY, which compiles Election Commission results). It may still come from official sources, but it is not published by the agency, so it needs review. A record takes the weakest tier among its listed source agencies. These tiers are a provisional definition by the prototype builder, not an official rating.

The index holds metadata only. It does not copy or read the values inside any dataset, and it cannot show live numbers. Fitness scores and answerability are estimates made by this tool from that metadata.

Some datasets list no geography even though their columns show a state or district breakdown. Those are marked inferred. Download and portal links follow each portal's catalogue address pattern. Always confirm on the official page before you cite a dataset. This is an independent prototype, not an official service of any agency.
